How to read this page
  1. Figures are annual, from the IMF's World Economic Outlook. Recent and future years are IMF estimates and projections, not outturns.
  2. The page shows the IMF's own series so that all eight economies use one definition. National statistics offices publish later or earlier releases and may differ, and revisions are routine.
  3. Fiscal-year economies (India reports on an April–March basis in its national accounts) may be calendarised differently by the IMF.
  4. "Taiwan" is shown as the IMF lists it, "Taiwan Province of China". "—" means the IMF has no value for that cell; nothing is estimated here.
  5. Monthly inflation is the 12-month change in the BIS consumer-price index for each economy's main national index, calculated here from BIS data. It is not seasonally adjusted and may differ from a national office's headline figure or release date.
  6. Policy rates are the BIS-reported main policy rate: India's repo rate, China's one-year Loan Prime Rate, Korea's base rate, Hong Kong's base rate, the mid-point of the Federal Reserve target range, and the ECB deposit facility rate. Taiwan's rate is the discount rate published by the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan). Singapore is not in the BIS policy-rate dataset. Taiwan has no monthly inflation figure here, because it is not in the BIS consumer-price dataset. Those cells are empty.
  7. Figures for current and future years are IMF estimates and projections and are revised often. Information only. Bono Juris is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or research analyst; nothing here is investment, legal or policy advice.
